## What is new in 1.8.4 (16 Nov 2026)
- **QuickBooks Online:** invoices whose lines came from another transaction (a delayed charge or billable time – the line shows a link icon) are now read in full, so their lines are no longer missing ("No invoice lines to send").

Also in 1.8.x: different home currencies (Zoho converts with its Central Bank rate), bill payments to Zoho, credit memos linked through payments, non-GCC customers for US companies.

Install: download PTG-Zoho-Sync-Setup-1.8.4.exe and run it (no administrator rights needed). It updates earlier versions in place; your profiles, keys and history are kept.

Windows SmartScreen may say *"Windows protected your PC"* because the installer isn't code-signed yet: click **More info → Run anyway**.
