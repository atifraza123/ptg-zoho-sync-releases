## What is new in 1.7.1 (13 Nov 2026)
- Every page scrolls (mouse wheel, or the scroll bar on the right) when it is taller than the window.
- Zoho Books *Test connection*: one clear line per link (connected / NOT connected and why), then the tools the sync still needs and what to do; long messages wrap to the window width.

Also in 1.7: QuickBooks Online as a source, QuickBooks Desktop 2009 to 2024+ (US, UK, Canadian editions; 32-bit and 64-bit QuickBooks), 32-bit app.

Install: download PTG-Zoho-Sync-Setup-1.7.1.exe and run it (no administrator rights needed). It updates earlier versions in place; your profiles, keys and history are kept.

Windows SmartScreen may say *"Windows protected your PC"* because the installer isn't code-signed yet: click **More info → Run anyway**.

To check the download (optional), compare its SHA-256 with the .sha256 file.
