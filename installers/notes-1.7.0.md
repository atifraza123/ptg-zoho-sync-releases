## What is new in 1.7.0 (13 Nov 2026)
- **QuickBooks Online** as a new source: invoices, credit memos, customers and payments go to Zoho Books; e-invoice status and supplier bills come back. Sign in with your Intuit developer app (Client ID / Client Secret); works with VAT companies (UAE, UK, ...) and US sales tax.
- **QuickBooks Desktop 2009 to 2024+**: the app agrees the qbXML version with each QuickBooks, reads UK / Canadian-style VAT codes and tax-inclusive invoices, and *Test connection* shows what your QuickBooks supports.
- The app is now **32-bit**, so it works with older 32-bit QuickBooks Desktop (2021 and earlier) as well as 64-bit 2022+. It runs on 32-bit and 64-bit Windows.
- Zoho MCP links: a link whose server no longer exists is shown as *NOT connected - address not found* with what to do.

Install: download PTG-Zoho-Sync-Setup-1.7.0.exe and run it (no administrator rights needed). It updates 1.6.0 in place; your profiles, keys and history are kept.

Windows SmartScreen may say *"Windows protected your PC"* because the installer isn't code-signed yet: click **More info → Run anyway**.

To check the download (optional), compare its SHA-256 with the .sha256 file.
