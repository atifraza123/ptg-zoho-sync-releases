## What is new in 1.8.3 (16 Nov 2026)
- **QuickBooks Online credit memos:** a credit memo applied to an invoice through a payment now finds that invoice, so it reaches Zoho with its associated invoice (required by UAE Zoho organizations). Credits applied show the credit memo's number.
- **"No invoice lines to send"** now names the line types the source sent, to identify unusual documents.

Install: download PTG-Zoho-Sync-Setup-1.8.3.exe and run it (no administrator rights needed). It updates earlier versions in place; your profiles, keys and history are kept.

Windows SmartScreen may say *"Windows protected your PC"* because the installer isn't code-signed yet: click **More info → Run anyway**.
